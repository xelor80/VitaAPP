<original_problem_statement>
The user wants to build a health-focused mobile app. Initially, the app was only in German, but the scope has been expanded to include Italian.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
1.  **Core Functionality:** Users input symptoms (free text) in either German or Italian. An LLM (OpenAI) analyzes the symptoms and provides:
    *   Evidence-based nutrition tips and recipes.
    *   General information on vitamins/supplements.
    *   Affiliate links to products from the Joachim Kaeser brand, using the appropriate German or Italian shop.
    *   For Italian products, display embedded YouTube videos where available.
2.  **Recipe Catalog:** Display a static, curated list of 30 recipes with high-quality, license-free images, in addition to the LLM-generated recipes.
3.  **Safety First:** The app must explicitly state it is not a medical device and direct users to medical professionals for serious symptoms (Red Flags). It needs clear disclaimers.
4.  **Monetization:** Primarily through affiliate link tracking and clicks.
5.  **Key User Journeys:**
    *   Symptom input -&gt; Results page (language-specific).
    *   Recipe details -&gt; Shopping list.
    *   Supplement info -&gt; Brand product page (affiliate link).
6.  **Symptom Diary:** A feature to track daily well-being (mood, sleep, etc.) and view trends, with LLM-provided lifestyle tips. This must also be internationalized.
7.  **Intake Schedule:** A feature to show a daily schedule for taking recommended supplements, using the official manufacturer instructions scraped from the product pages.
